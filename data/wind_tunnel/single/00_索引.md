# Wind-tunnel main experiment

One common-default row and nine single-definition perturbations. Each perturbed row changes exactly one of the 16 variable definitions, and factor records that definition.
The 100-row tiered set remains in data/wind_tunnel/batch and is not the entry for this main experiment.

- 000-001: single-definition perturbation: none. 207 blanks.
- S01: single-definition perturbation: theoretical angle of attack. factor `alpha`.
- S02: single-definition perturbation: theoretical sideslip. factor `beta`.
- S03: single-definition perturbation: theoretical roll. factor `gamma`.
- S04: single-definition perturbation: mechanism angle of attack. factor `alpha_m`.
- S05: single-definition perturbation: mechanism sideslip. factor `beta_m`.
- S06: single-definition perturbation: mechanism roll. factor `gamma_m`.
- S07: single-definition perturbation: installation. factor `placement`.
- S08: single-definition perturbation: elastic angle of attack. factor `alpha_e`.
- S09: single-definition perturbation: elastic sideslip. factor `beta_e`.
