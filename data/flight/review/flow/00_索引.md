# Flight-mechanics coordinate transforms

The main experiment is one standard definition and nine single-definition perturbations. Each item has 131 blanks, numbered 1 through 131. A single-definition perturbation changes one definition. The first four subprocesses convert coordinates. The last two convert the six aerodynamic components and transfer the moment reference point, and they are injected separately.

- [Data 01 common default](sample_01.md): single-definition perturbation: none. All five frames and all eight angles use the standard definition.
- [Data 02 yaw reversed](sample_02.md): single-definition perturbation: yaw. Yaw is negative when the nose yaws to the right of the ground.
- [Data 03 pitch reversed](sample_03.md): single-definition perturbation: pitch. Pitch is negative when the nose pitches above the ground.
- [Data 04 roll reversed](sample_04.md): single-definition perturbation: roll. Roll is negative when the aircraft rolls to its right.
- [Data 05 track inclination reversed](sample_05.md): single-definition perturbation: track inclination. Track inclination is negative when the aircraft climbs.
- [Data 06 angle of attack reversed](sample_06.md): single-definition perturbation: angle of attack. Angle of attack is negative when the airspeed projection lies below the airframe axis.
- [Data 07 sideslip reversed](sample_07.md): single-definition perturbation: sideslip. Sideslip is negative when the airspeed lies to the right of the aircraft.
- [Data 08 track azimuth reversed](sample_08.md): single-definition perturbation: track azimuth. Track azimuth is negative when the horizontal airspeed projection lies to the right of Xg.
- [Data 09 side force positive to the left](sample_09.md): single-definition perturbation: positive side force. Side force is positive along -Y_a of the airflow frame.
- [Data 10 lift positive downward](sample_10.md): single-definition perturbation: positive lift. Lift is positive along +Z_a of the airflow frame.
