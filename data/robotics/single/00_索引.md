# Robotics main experiment

One upright-wrist standard and nine single-definition perturbations. Each item changes one definition. Forward and inverse kinematics are still injected separately.
Samples that flip two or three joints stay in data/robotics/review/kinematics and are not part of this set.

- [Data 01 upright wrist](sample_01.md): single-definition perturbation: none.
- [Data 02 q1 sense reversed](sample_02.md): single-definition perturbation: joint angle q1.
- [Data 03 q2 sense reversed](sample_03.md): single-definition perturbation: joint angle q2.
- [Data 04 q3 sense reversed](sample_04.md): single-definition perturbation: joint angle q3.
- [Data 05 tool pitch reversed](sample_05.md): single-definition perturbation: tool pitch.
- [Data 06 tool lateral angle reversed](sample_06.md): single-definition perturbation: tool lateral angle.
- [Data 07 tool roll reversed](sample_07.md): single-definition perturbation: tool roll.
- [Data 08 leading-edge sweep reversed](sample_08.md): single-definition perturbation: leading-edge sweep.
- [Data 09 dihedral reversed](sample_09.md): single-definition perturbation: dihedral.
- [Data 10 wing-root coordinate reversed](sample_10.md): single-definition perturbation: wing-root coordinate.
